## Blind tests

Each month:
- Take 5 of your edits and 5 of Claude's, all in the same format, and shuffle them.
- Have someone who knows your work guess which are which.

Correct guesses near 50% means your style has transferred for that format. Near 100% means it hasn't yet, and their reasons tell us what to fix next.
