# Edit breakdown: [video title]

One of these per finished edit. The goal is to capture the **why** behind your decisions, and especially **the default you rejected**, because your style is the gap between the obvious choice and yours.

You don't need to log every cut. Log the 15 to 30 decisions you'd defend to another editor, especially the places where you:
- undid something,
- nudged something by a single frame,
- held longer than felt comfortable,
- or broke a rule on purpose.
